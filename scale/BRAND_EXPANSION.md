## Costing a brand out

The mechanics, not a scoring system. What the numbers *mean* is the discussion.

**Compute loaded cost twice — the two stacks differ.** Start from a buy-cost
*range* (40–50% of observed RRP; don't trust a single point), then load it:

- **Amazon-loaded:** buy + VAT + Amazon referral (~15%) + FBA + DPD + wages +
  storage.
- **Shopify-loaded:** buy + VAT + our own fulfilment + DPD + wages + storage —
  no referral, no FBA. Materially lower.

Report against the pessimistic (50%) buy cost, so "works even if we negotiate
badly" is distinguishable from "only works on hard terms".

**Read the live listing, not the RRP.** For each hero style: current buy-box
price, seller count and who they are (brand/Amazon-direct vs grey EU
arbitrage), and whether the brand is discounting its own product. On Shopify we
set our own price, so the question there is whether a price that clears
Shopify-loaded cost is competitive with the wider market.

**Shopify has a cost the stack doesn't show — acquisition.** Amazon hands you
the buyer for the referral fee, which is already in the Amazon stack. Shopify
traffic is bought with Google Ads or earned with SEO, and none of that is in the
Shopify-loaded number. Branded search volume is the usual proxy for how cheaply
a brand's traffic can be had.

**Demand reference points**, for reading a branded-search number against
something real:

| Brand | UK branded searches/mo | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Birkenstock | 240K | Moat/monopoly; our Shopify engine |
| Rieker | 9.9K | We own it; Amazon-leaning |
| Free Spirit | 1.0K | We stock it; partly generic search |

For an Amazon read, Amazon's own demand signals (BSR, review count) are the
relevant ones — Google volume speaks to the Shopify case.

**Both channels before dropping anything.** A brand that doesn't work on Amazon
has failed one configuration, not the brand — the fees and the price you're
pinned to are different on Shopify. Equally, a clean Shopify margin isn't a
result on its own until acquisition cost is in the picture.
